Week one takes you from the raw payment stream to the board-level MRR report. You'll learn the six tables that matter, how revenue and churn are defined, which pipelines feed them, the SQL analysts actually run, and the three people who own it all.

## Day 1: Core tables to understand

- **payments — raw payment attempts** — The Stripe webhook stream. Includes failures and retries; always filter to status='succeeded' for revenue work. (`urn:li:dataset:(urn:li:dataPlatform:postgres,northbeam_app.public.payments,PROD)`)
- **fct_revenue — the canonical revenue fact** — One row per successful payment, net of refunds. Use net_amount_usd. (`urn:li:dataset:(urn:li:dataPlatform:snowflake,analytics.marts.fct_revenue,PROD)`)
- **mrr_monthly — board-level rollup** — Monthly MRR/ARR by plan; feeds the Finance KPIs dashboard. (`urn:li:dataset:(urn:li:dataPlatform:snowflake,analytics.marts.mrr_monthly,PROD)`)

## Day 2: Key metrics & glossary terms

- **MRR** — Sum of active subscription amounts normalized monthly; annual plans divided by 12.
- **Churn Rate** — churned_subscribers / subscribers_at_period_start, on calendar months.
- **GMV vs net revenue** — GMV is pre-refund order value; revenue reporting uses net_amount_usd.

## Day 3: Important pipelines & lineage

- **payments → stg_payments → fct_revenue → mrr_monthly** — the revenue spine.
- **subscriptions + dim_customers → fct_churn** — check both sides when churn looks off.
- **payment_health_daily** — the on-call view when payment alarms fire.

## Day 4: Common SQL patterns

- **Monthly MRR trend by plan** — the saved query finance uses for the board deck.
- **Revenue-by-geo** — fct_revenue joined to dim_customers on customer_id.
- **Churn with 3-month rolling average** — window-function pattern over fct_churn.
